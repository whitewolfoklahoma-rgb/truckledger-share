Which events are worth rebooking?

Recurring stops are grouped by name. Verdicts compare average profit per hour against your target (set it in Help & Backup — the default is $40/hr): at or above target = Rebook, within 25% under = Borderline, below that = Skip. A big-sales event that eats 10 hours can still be a bad event — profit per hour is the honest number.

Weekly food-cost worksheet

The worksheet the trade swears by, done weekly so a cost creep shows up in days, not at month-end: Food cost = beginning inventory + purchases − ending inventory, and Food-cost % = food cost ÷ food sales. Target band is set in Help & Backup (default 27–32%).

How TruckLedger works

Events are the heart of it. Log every service with its sales and its real costs; TruckLedger returns profit and profit per hour. Use the same event name for recurring stops ("Downtown Lunch") and the Rankings tab will average them, so one rainy Tuesday doesn't fool you.

Food cost is a weekly habit: count inventory, add purchases, count again. Ten minutes a week beats a surprise at month-end — by the time a monthly P&L shows food cost creeping, you're four to six weeks too late.

Menu costing keeps the menu honest: price from plate cost, not from what the truck two spots down charges.
